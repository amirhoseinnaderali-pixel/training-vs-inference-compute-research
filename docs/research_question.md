# Research question

Primary question: Under a fixed total compute envelope, how does reallocating compute between model training and inference-time computation change objective held-out correctness?

H1: the response to allocation is measurable and may be nonlinear.
H2: training and inference compute are not assumed interchangeable.
H0: paired allocation changes do not produce systematic hidden-correctness differences beyond sampling variation.

No hypothesis designates a preferred allocation.
